# Image manifest

The training page looks for these files in this folder. Until a file is here the
page shows a labelled placeholder in its slot, so nothing breaks while they are
missing.

Save each image you pasted into chat under the exact filename below. Any of
these work: right click the image in the source page and Save image as, or open
the PDF and export the image.

| `thirds-bird.jpg` | Blue and orange bird on a branch with the rule of thirds grid drawn on |

| `dof-portrait.jpg` | Woman sitting in grass, Aperture f/2 on the left and f/22 on the right |
